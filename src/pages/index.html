Welcome

Where heritage is slowly made

For generations, Korean households have trusted the onggi, the breathing earthenware jar, to transform simple ingredients into something extraordinary. La Kimchi is built on the same belief: that true quality cannot be rushed.

Our forty-two suites, acclaimed restaurant and restorative spa are designed around that idea, offering an unhurried, deeply personal stay in one of Asia's most dynamic cities.

The Residence

Architecture shaped
by tradition

Designed by architect Baek Chu-Il, La Kimchi's tower draws its silhouette from the onggi jar. Terracotta-toned facades, hand-thrown ceramics and natural ventilation keep the building calm, quiet and connected to the hanok neighbourhood around it.

Inside, every suite pairs Korean materials, including hanji paper, walnut and celadon, with discreet modern comfort.
