Welcome

Where opulence meets lacto-fermentation

For centuries, the great houses of Korea have entrusted their finest cabbages to the patient darkness of the onggi jar. We simply asked: what if the cabbage were you?

At La Kimchi, every guest is gently salted upon arrival, lovingly layered with the softest Egyptian cotton, and left to develop complexity in rooms maintained at a constant, flattering 4°C. You will leave transformed. Tangier. Better.

The Residence

Forty-two suites.
One very large jar.

Designed by the reclusive architect Baek Chu-Il, La Kimchi's central tower takes the form of a 300-metre ceramic onggi, partially buried in the earth to maintain the ideal temperature for both guests and radishes.

Each suite breathes through the porous terracotta walls, allowing a gentle exchange of air, ideas and garlic.

Signature Experiences

The art of slow living

Salting Ceremony

Upon arrival, a white-gloved salt sommelier seasons you with fleur de sel harvested from the tears of a disappointed Michelin inspector.

Brine Butler

Available around the clock to top up your brine, turn down your bed, and gently press you to remove air pockets.

Gochugaru Turndown

Forget mints on the pillow. Each evening, we leave a single, hand-selected chili flake. You will never find it again.

Burp Service

Twice daily, a member of staff opens your door for exactly four seconds to release built-up gases. This is not optional.
