About the hotel

Comfort in the centre of Seoul

La Kimchi is located in Jongno-gu, a short walk from Gyeongbokgung Palace, Insadong and Anguk subway station. It's a convenient base for both business and leisure travellers.

Every room has a comfortable bed, a modern bathroom, fast Wi-Fi and city views. Our staff are available 24 hours a day to help with anything you need.
