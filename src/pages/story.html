Heritage

Built on
patience.

La Kimchi began with Kim Chae-Ri, a hotelier who grew up among her grandmother's onggi jars in a Bukchon courtyard. She believed the values behind those jars, patience, care and respect for craft, could define a new kind of luxury hotel.

Opened in 2019, La Kimchi brings that vision to life: a hotel where Korean artisans, chefs and designers shape every part of the guest experience.

Our Philosophy

Time is the ultimate luxury.

We believe the finest experiences, like the finest flavours, develop with time. That means attentive service without hurry, spaces that invite you to slow down, and a commitment to the craftspeople who keep Korean traditions alive.

Each year, La Kimchi supports local ceramicists, textile makers and farmers through our Artisan Residency programme, and many of their works can be found throughout the hotel.
