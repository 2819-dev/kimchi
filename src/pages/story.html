Heritage

A story told
very slowly.

In the winter of 2019, Madame Kim Chae-Ri — heiress, cabbage enthusiast, and the only person ever banned from the Ritz for “excessive pungency” — stood in her grandmother's courtyard and looked at the family's ancient onggi jars.

“These jars,” she reportedly said, “have better climate control than any hotel I have ever stayed in.” Then she climbed into one. She came out three weeks later with a business plan and an extremely good complexion.

Our Philosophy

Patience is the ultimate luxury.

Anyone can build a hotel with marble lobbies and gold taps. (We have those too. The taps dispense brine.) What we offer is rarer: time. Time to soften. Time to deepen. Time for the good bacteria to do their work.

We do not rush. We do not hurry. Room service takes between twenty minutes and six weeks, depending on what you ordered and how ripe it needs to be.

La Kimchi is entirely fictional. Please do not attempt to check in to a jar.
