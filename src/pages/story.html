About Us

Our hotel

A family-run hotel since 2019

La Kimchi opened in 2019 as an independent, family-owned hotel. Our goal is simple: comfortable rooms, good food and friendly, helpful service.

We work with local suppliers wherever we can, and our team is always happy to recommend restaurants, shops and sights around the city.

Location

Getting here
